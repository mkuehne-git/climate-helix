Each chart below plots the annual mean temperature difference to a reference mean, for one NASA GISS dataset snapshot [2]. The three lines are the Global, Northern Hemisphere and Southern Hemisphere regions shown by the helix.

Scroll to see one chart per dataset snapshot. Hover a chart to read exact values for a given year.

Use the year range slider to zoom the x-axis of all charts at once; the y-axis fits the years in view.
